lesson  = Arrays: a list of numbers, one name, an index
chapter = 3

[lines]
' Add up the charge in the three cells
DIM cell(3)
cell(1) = 10 : cell(2) = 20 : cell(3) = 30
total = 0
FOR k = 1 TO 3
    total = total + cell(k)
NEXT
PRINT total
[blanks]
2 DIM
6 total
[hints]
DIM makes the array before it's used
total starts at 0, and each cell is added to it
